## Historical Context: What are Trade Cards?
Before we delve into the enchanting world of sneaky elves, let’s talk a bit about trade cards. Trade cards were all the rage in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Imagine them as the Instagram posts of their time—companies distributed these colorful cards to promote their products or services. People collected them, traded them, and even used them as bookmarks. Talk about multi-tasking!

### Early 20th Century Marketing
In 1923, the world was experiencing the aftermath of World War I, and marketing was shifting into high gear. The N.E. Dairy & Food Council embraced this change, aiming to promote dairy products through fun and engaging imagery. What better way to do that than with mischievous little elves causing a ruckus while children sleep? They knew how to catch a parent's eye!

## Collecting Trade Cards: The How and Why
Now that we’ve frolicked through the enchanting world of sneaky elves, let’s talk about the practical side of these trade cards. How can you get your hands on one? And why should you even bother?

### Why Collect Trade Cards?
1. **History:** These cards are not just collectibles; they’re wee time machines whisking you back to a more innocent age.
2. **Artistic Value:** The illustrations are often done by artists who were masters of their craft, making these cards collectible art pieces in their own right.
3. **Community:** The trade card community is both niche and passionate. Finding fellow enthusiasts can lead to exciting trade opportunities and friendships.

### How to Start Your Collection
- **Research:** Start by familiarizing yourself with different categories and eras of trade cards. There’s a bit of everything out there!
- **Join Online Communities:** Platforms such as Reddit have great communities where collectors share tips and trade cards themselves.
- **Local Shows:** Keep an eye out for local antique or collector shows where you can find trade cards.
