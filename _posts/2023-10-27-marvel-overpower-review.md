## What is Marvel OverPower?

Marvel OverPower is a collectible card game created by **West End Games** and was the brainchild of a dream team that included comic book legends. The idea? Let’s collide all of Marvel’s heroes and get them to duke it out on the tabletop! 

### Card Game Mechanics: Let’s Power Up!

One of the most unique aspects of OverPower was its basic gameplay mechanics. Here’s how it worked:
- **Deck Building:** Players would create decks of cards with different abilities. It’s like a superhero Tinder where you match your skills with whatever powers suit your style!
- **Strengths and Weaknesses:** Just like in real life, every hero had strengths and weaknesses.  But don’t worry—nobody has to cry about getting picked last in gym class!
- **Combat:** Players took turns drawing cards and using their abilities to overpower opponents. It was a cerebral game, testing your brain against rival masterminds trying to mundane-ify your joy.

Guys, this wasn’t just a mindless shuffle of cards. This was a battle of wits, strategy, and a good sprinkling of luck.

### Where Did It All Go Wrong?

Alas, like many glories from the past, Marvel OverPower met its end. The game was discontinued in the double-dare decade of the 2000s. Like a beloved television series ending abruptly (we're looking at you, *Firefly*), fans were left reeling and challenged to find solutions to continue their hero-based battles. What happened? 

Maybe it all came down to a lack of new releases or fierce competition from other TCGs (we see you, Pokémon). In the melee of card games, OverPower struggled to stay afloat. But don't worry! It never left our hearts!
