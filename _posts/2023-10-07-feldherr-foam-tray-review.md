## The Great Debate: To Paint or Not to Paint?

While you’re organizing your miniatures, you might ask yourself whether to paint them or not. Miniatures can look bare as a cat in a bathtub without paint. Here’s a fun fact: Studies show that painted miniatures roll better for high scores (results may vary)!  

### Painting Tips  
- **Brush Type**: Use a quality brush, or your details may end up looking like a toddler’s crayon drawing.  
- **Start Small**: Get comfortable with a few miniatures before tackling that monstrous ogre.
- **Sealing Your Work**: Don’t forget to seal your painted miniatures to protect them! It’ll ensure that your masterpiece doesn’t become a mess.
