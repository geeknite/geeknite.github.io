# Michelin Wild AM2 Enduro Review: A Tire Made for Legends

Let’s face it, if you’re riding the trails these days, you need more than just a bike. You need the right tires—tread that grips like a toddler on a beach ball and rolling resistance low enough to put a tortoise to shame. Enter the Michelin Wild AM2, the latest offering from one of the giants in the tire universe. 

This tire aims to strike that beautiful balance between performance and durability that every rider dreams about, especially when tackling those gnarly enduro trails that make your limbs feel like jello. Let’s dig deep, shall we?

## A Wild Beginning

Before we dive into the features, you might be wondering why it’s called the 'Wild AM2.' Let me tell you, it’s not just a catchy name; it’s practically a tribute to the untamed spirit of mountain biking. Michelin designed this tire for a variety of terrains, which means it has to be as versatile as your cousin at family reunions—ready to adapt but capable of bringing the heat when needed.

## Tire Features that Make You Go "Whoa!"

### 1. **Tread Design**

The tread pattern on the Wild AM2 is what dreams are made of, if your dreams involve standing on a mountain bike and shouting ‘YEAH!’ at the top of your lungs. The aggressive knobs are designed to bite into all surfaces, offering grip that would make a grizzly bear envious.

### 2. **Compound Technology**

Michelin hasn’t skimped on the rubber. The Wild AM2 sports their latest rubber compound that blends grip with durability. It’s like the perfect marriage between a soft pillow and a sturdy brick—soft enough to absorb shocks but tough enough to take a beating. It’s basically Hulk Hogan, but in tire form.

### 3. **Size Options**

Available in a variety of sizes ranging from 27.5” to 29”, these tires have you covered, whether you’re a fan of the sausage roll look or just trying to embrace the ‘bigger is better’ philosophy. You can virtually see those rolling hills calling your name! 

## Installation – Easy-Peasy or a Nightmare? 

So how easy is it to slap these bad boys on your bike? My personal experience was smooth—like butter on warm toast. The bead seated perfectly, and I didn’t need to sacrifice my first-born to inflation gods, which is always a plus. A word of advice, though: for the best experience, use a tire lever. Trust me; it’ll save you a lot of blood, sweat, and tears.

## On the Trail: Grip, Grip, and More Grip

Time to get our hands dirty; let’s talk performance. Out on the trails, the Wild AM2 was like a raccoon at a dumpster—fearless and ready to tackle anything. The traction is remarkable; whether you’re climbing steep, rocky inclines or ripping through muddy sections, these tires grip like they owe you money. 

### **Cornering**

Cornering was a delight. The tires hold their line like a sworn bodyguard, unwavering even at sharp angles. I may or may not have embraced the phrase, 'Hold my beer' as I pushed my limits on those winding trails. Just a friendly reminder: I don’t recommend trying that at home. 

### **Durability**

Let’s pull out the crystal ball and talk longevity. After several outings—which, let's be honest, turned into mud wrestling contests with dirt and rocks—the Wild AM2 tires showed minimal wear. The knobs retained their form, proving that Michelin really took the phrase 'built to last' seriously.

## Pricing: Worth the Investment or Just a Fancy Pizza Cutter?

Alright, let’s address the elephant in the room—pricing. The Michelin Wild AM2 tires hover around the sweet spot of $70 to $80 per tire, depending on where you shop. It’s not pocket change, but considering the performance and durability, it feels like a solid investment rather than a splurge. It’s definitely better than the alternative: buying cheap tires that leave you stranded on the trails with only the squirrels for company.
