## Painting Tips for Your Bandits
  
### Gather Your Supplies
  
Trading your gaming time for painting time doesn’t require a PhD in fine art. All you need is a basic set of miniature paint brushes, acrylic paints, and perhaps a steady hand (or several shaky ones, no judgment). Here’s a quick checklist:  
- Miniature paints (preferably acrylic)  
- A fine brush for details  
- A larger brush for covering surfaces  
- Palette for mixing colors  
- A steady surface (or a wobbly table, we don’t judge)  
  
### Priming Your Miniatures
  
Before you get too carried away, don’t forget to prime your miniatures! Priming not only helps paint adhere better but also ensures your colors pop. **Spray primer** is a fast way to go (just make sure you take it outside unless you fancy smelling like spray paint for days)!  
  
### Techniques & Tricks
  
- **Basecoat**: Start with a basecoat of your chosen color for each section (think of it like painting your tiny house!). 
- **Dry Brushing**: For highlights, use dry brushing techniques to gently layer lighter colors on raised areas. 
- **Wash**: Use a wash (diluted paint) in the crevices for a more dynamic look. You’ll be amazed at how this adds depth!  
  
### Be Brave, Experiment! 
  
Don’t be too hard on yourself! Experiment with wild color choices, and embrace imperfections. Every bandit has a story, and a paint splotch can add unique character. Trust us, your players will never claim to have met a bandit with a seemingly mismatched sock (in fact, they might love it)!
