## Gameplay Mechanics: Not Just for Collectors
### An Overview of How to Play
_In classic card game style_, OverPower doesn’t just sit there; it demands some serious tactics and brainstorm-heavy moves to ensure victory is yours. Whether you’re a strategic mastermind or just remembering to draw cards, there’s room for everyone.
Here’s a quick overview of gameplay:
1. **Setup**: Shuffle your deck and draw your hand. Make sure you’ve got your snacks at arm’s reach—strategic snacking is key!
2. **Take Turns**: Players alternate taking turns to play cards and countering the moves of their opponents. Nothing like a surprise attack to really shake things up!  
3. **Abilities Activation**: Utilize your cards’ unique powers to either attack or defend. Rogue would be proud!
4. **Victory Conditions**: Typically, you lose if your hero is knocked out. So keeping that health up while dishing out damage is the name of the game.

### Strategizing With Rogue
